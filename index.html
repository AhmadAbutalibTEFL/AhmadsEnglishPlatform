Learn the Seven Parts of Speech

Every word has a job in a sentence. The job tells you its part of speech.

How to Identify a Part of Speech

Don't identify a word by itself. Look at the word in the sentence.

1

Read the whole sentence

Read the sentence first. Look at the complete idea.

2

Look at the target word

Look at the word and the words around it.

3

Ask the key question

“What job does this word do?”

4

Choose the part of speech

Use the word's job in the sentence to identify it.

Let's try one

The students study every evening.

Target word: study

What job does study do?

It shows an action.

Therefore, study is a verb.
