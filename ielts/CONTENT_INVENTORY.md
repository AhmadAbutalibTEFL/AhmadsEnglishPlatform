## Before any third-party material is published

1. Identify the exact resource (title, edition, test, part, track).
2. Record who owns it and the licence or written permission, including whether **online** use is allowed.
3. Open the real files and map audio, questions and answer key by content, not filename.
4. Only then change `verified` and `publishPermission` in the data and update this table.

## External format verification

- IELTS official Academic Listening format: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening
- IELTS official General Training Listening format: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-listening
- IELTS announced in March 2026 that paper-test availability would change from mid-2026, with exact timelines varying by market: https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery. Therefore, the website avoids implying paper tests are universally available.
