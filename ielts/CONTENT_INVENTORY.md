## Before any third-party material is published

1. Identify the exact resource (title, edition, test, part, track).
2. Record who owns it and the licence or written permission, including whether **online** use is allowed.
3. Open the real files and map audio, questions and answer key by content, not filename.
4. Only then change `verified` and `publishPermission` in the data and update this table.
